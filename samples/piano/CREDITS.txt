Piano samples
=============

The files in this folder (A0.mp3 ... C8.mp3) are from the
Salamander Grand Piano by Alexander Holm, a recording of a Yamaha C5 grand piano.

License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 (CC BY 3.0)
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

These MP3 versions (one velocity layer, one note every minor third from A0 to C8)
were taken from the Tone.js audio collection:
https://tonejs.github.io/audio/salamander/

Keystage plays them back pitch-shifted, filtered by velocity, and mixed with its own
effects. The samples are not covered by Keystage's MIT license; they remain under
CC BY 3.0 as stated above.
